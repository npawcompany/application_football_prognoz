# News samples (synthetic)

All files are synthetic and used only by tests. They follow the documented formats:

- `gnews_search.json`, `gnews_error_quota.json` — GNews v4 `/api/v4/search` response and 403 error body (https://docs.gnews.io).
- `rss_bbc.xml`, `rss_guardian.xml` — RSS 2.0 in the same structure as the live BBC Sport and Guardian football feeds (checked 2026-09-29). Headlines are invented.
